# Story decisions (delegated to Claude by the creator)

These settle the five issues raised in the brief review. They apply from Stage 4 onwards; none
are implemented in Stage 1.

1. **One storm explains everything.** A storm the night before the player arrives broke the
   footbridge, washed Rabbit's kettle and Mouse's chest onto the far bank, lodged the brass button
   in the reeds, knocked over the last trail marker, and is why the player got lost. The opening
   text can hint at it.
2. **Mouse's chest is too heavy for a mouse.** Mouse waits at the near bank before the repair.
   Once the bridge is fixed she tries, can't shift the chest, and goes back to the village oak to
   find help. That's why the human player carries it to her.
3. **Magpie is a cheerful collector, not a gatekeeper.** She found the adze, thinks it's a fine
   curiosity, and swaps it gladly for something shinier. She'd give it back if asked, but she
   *offers* the trade.
4. **The seeds become the missing trail marker.** Hedgehog grows glow-moss that shines at dusk.
   The spare seeds let her plant a glowing row at the uncertain turn. This actually solves the
   navigation problem: once it's planted, the player can leave alone. Walking out with Hedgehog
   is an optional, reassuring ending.
5. **One remembered choice.** When the player returns the kettle, Rabbit offers a choice of
   tea: mint or bramble. Badger later comments on it (he's partial to bramble). This is small and
   optional, but it exercises a remembered choice with a later consequence before the generated
   milestone depends on one.
